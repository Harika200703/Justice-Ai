# Justice AI scope
Primary workflow: upload a legal document; return a summary, potentially relevant Indian central/state laws and sections, constitutional Articles when supported, potential consequences only when grounded in exact provisions, rules, evidence gaps, next steps, sources and uncertainty.
Reliability: never invent citations or punishments. Verify current wording, amendments, commencement, jurisdiction and state rules. Source-discovery links do not mean underlying law was retrieved or validated.
Secondary feature: SHA-256 and optional Solidity evidence registry; a matching hash does not prove a document is true or admissible.
Before production: ingest a versioned official legal corpus with retrieval citations; add OCR, retention/deletion controls, rate limits, audit logs, malware scanning, HTTPS, backups and legal/security review.
